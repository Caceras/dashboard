Keyword research isn't about finding terms — it's about finding terms **the client can realistically rank for and that move their business**. Most lists fail one of those two tests.

## Step 1 — Define the business goal

Before opening Ahrefs or SEMrush, write the answer to: *"If this campaign hits, what changes in the client's bank account?"* If the answer is fuzzy, stop and re-scope.

## Step 2 — Build the seed list

20-40 phrases, sourced from:

- The client's own product pages and FAQ.
- Their top three competitors' titles and H1s.
- Real customer support transcripts (the gold mine).
- Reddit and niche forum threads where their customers actually hang out.

Don't use AI suggestions until step 4. They produce plausible-sounding keywords that no one searches for.

## Step 3 — Score each candidate

For every seed, capture in `/seo`:

| Metric | Threshold for "track this" |
| --- | --- |
| `search_volume` | ≥ 200/mo for B2C, ≥ 50/mo for B2B |
| `difficulty` | ≤ client's domain rating + 10 |
| Intent match | The page that ranks #1 today is the same kind of page we'd build |

Any candidate that fails two of three: cut it.

## Step 4 — Cluster, then prioritize

Group survivors into 3-5 clusters by topic. Prioritize the cluster where:

- Competition has gaps (top 10 has weak pages).
- Your client has unique authority (case studies, data, expertise).
- Conversion intent is highest (transactional &gt; informational at the start).

## Step 5 — Track in Works

Every kept keyword goes into `/seo` with:

- `client_id` — so the page can filter.
- `domain` — exactly the domain you're tracking.
- `country` — never assume US.
- `position` and `previous_position` — set by the weekly check.

The Today page surfaces the biggest movers automatically. The Friday review is when you decide what to do about them.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-target"}
**Heuristic:** If a keyword hasn't moved in 90 days and isn't in the top 20, it's probably not worth the slot. Demote or replace.
::

## Anti-patterns

- **Tracking 500 keywords.** You can't act on 500. Start with 20.
- **Volume worship.** A 10-volume keyword that converts at 8% beats a 10,000-volume keyword that converts at 0.1%.
- **Ignoring intent.** Ranking #1 for a keyword whose top results are all blog posts when you have a product page is a wasted slot.
