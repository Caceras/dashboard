Before sending a proposal, run this audit on the client's domain. It tells you whether the project is winnable, where the early wins live, and what to charge.

## Quick crawl (5 min)

- Robots.txt isn't blocking anything important.
- Sitemap exists and is fresh (last-modified within 30 days).
- 200/301/404 ratio is healthy. &gt; 5% 4xx is a red flag.
- HTTPS, no mixed content.
- One canonical version of the homepage (no `www` vs. apex split).

## Performance (5 min)

Run Lighthouse on the homepage and one product/service page. Capture in `/seo` as a `seo_audit` row:

- `performance` — LCP &lt; 2.5s, CLS &lt; 0.1, INP &lt; 200ms.
- `best_practices` — no console errors, no broken HTTPS.
- `accessibility` — alt text, contrast, focus states.
- `seo` — meta tags, structured data, mobile-friendly.

Anything &lt; 70 in any category is a billable fix.

## Content (10 min)

- Pick the top three commercial pages. Each should:
  - Have a unique, intent-matching `<title>` &lt; 60 chars.
  - Have a unique meta description that previews the page.
  - Use one H1, in plain language a customer would say.
  - Link to at least three other pages on the site.

## Backlinks (5 min)

In Ahrefs/SEMrush:

- Domain Rating relative to top three competitors.
- New referring domains in the last 90 days.
- Toxic links flagged for disavow.
- Anchor text distribution. &gt; 30% exact-match commercial = penalty risk.

## Technical (3 min)

- Schema.org markup on key pages (Product, Article, LocalBusiness — whichever applies).
- `hreflang` if international.
- Pagination handled correctly.
- JavaScript-rendered content is in the rendered HTML.

## Competitor gaps (2 min)

In a content gap report, find the top 10 keywords competitors rank for that the client doesn't. Three of these usually become quick-win blog briefs in the proposal.

## Producing the score

The aggregate `score` on `/seo` audits is the average of the four Lighthouse categories minus a penalty per `issues` count. Anything &lt; 60 is a "rebuild not refurbish" project. 60–80 is the sweet spot for retainer work. 80+ means the easy wins are gone — propose link-building or content expansion.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-stopwatch"}
**Time check:** This whole audit fits in 30 minutes. If it takes longer, you're going too deep for a pre-pitch. Save the deep dive for after the contract.
::
