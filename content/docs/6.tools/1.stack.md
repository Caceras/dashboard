Tools cost money and attention. The list in `/tools` is short on purpose. Every entry below has earned its slot.

## SEO

- **Ahrefs** — keyword research, backlinks, competitor gap analysis. Non-negotiable.
- **Screaming Frog** — site audits at scale. Annual license; pays for itself in two audits.
- **SEMrush** — kept around as a sanity check on Ahrefs data.

If we had to drop one: SEMrush. The data overlap with Ahrefs is too high.

## Design & content

- **Figma** — design source of truth. Files are the deliverable for brand work.
- **Notion** — internal docs for things outside Works (long-form research, client wikis).

## Engineering & hosting

- **Vercel** — frontends, API routes, deployments. Pro plan for the team-features.
- **Cloudflare** — DNS, edge cache, image resizing, security.
- **GitHub** — code, issues, code review.

## Project & comms

- **Linear** — internal task tracking. Issues link out to the Works project they belong to.
- **Loom** — async video for design reviews. Replaces 80% of "got 15 minutes?" calls.
- **Google Workspace** — email and calendar source of truth. Calendar events sync into `/calendar`.

## Finance & legal

- **Stripe** — invoicing and recurring billing.
- **1Password** — credentials shared with contractors safely.

## Picking a new tool

Before adding anything to `/tools`, answer:

1. **What does this replace?** If nothing, it's adding cognitive load, not removing it.
2. **Will I still use it in 6 months?** Be honest. Trial it for two weeks first.
3. **What's the all-in cost?** Subscription + setup time + the cost of switching back.

If two of three are unclear, don't subscribe.

## When to cancel

The monthly ritual reconciles tool burn against revenue. Anything under **5% utilization** for two months in a row gets cancelled. The exception is annual seats — let them lapse, don't refund-fight.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-circle-x" color="warning"}
**The 8% rule:** Total tool burn shouldn't exceed 8% of monthly revenue. When it does, you're tooling for an aspiration, not a reality.
::
