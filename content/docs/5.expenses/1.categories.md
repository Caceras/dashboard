Every expense in `/expenses` belongs in exactly one category. The point is that **future-you** can answer "what did we spend on contractors last quarter?" in five seconds.

## The eight categories

### `tools`

Recurring software bills. Anything that appears in `/tools` should not also appear here — that's a reconciliation error. Use `tools` for one-off purchases: a font license, a Stock photo pack you bought once.

### `contractors`

Humans you paid for project-specific work. Always set `project_id` and `billable: true` if it's pass-through.

### `assets`

Stock photos, fonts, sound effects, licensed images. Almost always tied to a specific project (`project_id`).

### `domains`

Domain registrations and DNS services. Often `billable: true` for client domains.

### `workspace`

Coworking, office space, conference rooms, even a coffee shop you bought a drink at to take a meeting. Rarely billable.

### `meetings`

The meal, the coffee, the Uber to a client meeting. `client_id` set when applicable. Reimbursability follows your contract.

### `travel`

Flights, hotels, ground transit. Almost always tied to `client_id` and billable per contract.

### `other`

The category of last resort. If "other" creeps over 10% of monthly spend, you have a missing category — propose adding it instead of widening the bucket.

## The five required fields

| Field | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `description` | A future-you-readable sentence, not a vendor name. "Stock photos for Acme rebrand" not "Adobe". |
| `vendor` | Who you actually paid. Used for vendor-level reporting. |
| `category` | One of the eight above. |
| `project_id` / `client_id` | Whichever applies. Both can be set. |
| `billable` | True if you can pass this to a client per contract. |

## Reimbursement loop

`billable: true` + `reimbursed: false` after 7 days = invoice now. The Today page surfaces this automatically. The longer you wait, the more questions a client has.

## Why this matters at year-end

If categories are clean, your accountant's bill drops by half and your tax-deductible total stops surprising you. That's the whole point.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-receipt-text" color="info"}
**Tip:** When in doubt between two categories, pick the one you'd want to **filter on later**. That's the right one.
::
