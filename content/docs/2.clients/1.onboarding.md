A client moves through four statuses in `/clients`: `prospect` → `active` → `paused` → `archived`. The transition from `prospect` to `active` is the one that matters most. Get this right and the next twelve months are calm.

## The six steps

### 1. Discovery call (30 minutes, free)

Goal: confirm there's a real problem worth paying for. Listen more than you pitch. Status stays `prospect`.

### 2. Scoping doc (24-hour turnaround)

Write a one-page doc with three sections: **what we'll do**, **what we won't do**, **what success looks like**. Send it from `/clients` → "Send scope" with a 7-day expiry.

### 3. Proposal (max two options)

Two pricing options only. A third option means you don't believe in either of the first two. Tag the client `proposal-sent` in `/clients` so weekly review surfaces stale proposals.

### 4. Contract

Use the [contract checklist](/docs/clients/contracts) — every clause on that page is non-negotiable. Move status to `active` only after the contract is countersigned, not before.

### 5. Kickoff

Create the kickoff event in `/calendar` with `kind: meeting`. Link it to the new project in `/projects`. The event description is the agenda — write it the day before, not in the meeting.

### 6. First invoice

Sent within 7 days of kickoff. If you can't invoice in 7 days, you didn't scope it tightly enough. Status `active` requires a paid invoice on file.

## What to capture in the client record

| Field | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| `hourly_rate` | Drives the budget warning on `/projects`. |
| `tags` | Surface high-value, retainer, lead, etc. on the table view. |
| `location` | Time zone for scheduling, jurisdiction for the contract. |
| `email` | The one address that gets every invoice and report. |

::callout{icon="i-lucide-circle-alert" color="warning"}
Don't set `status: active` without a signed contract and a paid first invoice. The pipeline lies if this rule is broken.
::

## When to archive

A client moves to `archived` when both are true:

- No active project for 90 days.
- No invoice issued in the last 180 days.

Archived clients still appear in expense and project history but stop polluting the active count on `/`.
