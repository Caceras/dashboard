Works is calm by design. The home page surfaces what needs your attention; the rituals below keep the data behind it trustworthy.

## The daily ritual

Five minutes, every morning, in this order:

1. **Open `/`** and scan the **Pipeline** ribbon. Anything in `Blocked` is the day's first conversation.
2. **Look at "Needs attention".** Overdue and at-risk projects bubble to the top automatically — clear or escalate, don't scroll past.
3. **Glance at the Agenda.** Confirm the next event has a location and a participant.
4. **Log expenses from yesterday.** If you can't remember what something was for, it didn't deserve to be billed.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-zap"}
**Shortcut:** Hit `g h` from anywhere to jump back to Today.
::

## The weekly ritual

Friday afternoon, twenty minutes:

- **Reposition projects.** If `progress` and `deadline` disagree, move the deadline before the project moves itself.
- **Sweep `/seo`.** Anything that fell more than five positions gets a ticket. Anything that gained more than five gets celebrated in the client report.
- **Reconcile billable expenses.** If something is `billable: true` but `reimbursed: false` after seven days, invoice it now.
- **Renewals.** `/tools` shows what renews in the next thirty days. Cancel the trials before they auto-charge.

## The monthly ritual

First business day, one hour:

1. **Audit each active client** in `/clients`. If you haven't shipped or invoiced this month, schedule a check-in.
2. **Run a fresh audit** on every client domain in `/seo`. The score trend matters more than the absolute number.
3. **Reconcile** total tool burn against revenue. Anything under 5% utilization gets cancelled or downgraded.
4. **Plan next month's calendar.** Block focus time before client meetings claim it.

## What good looks like

- Pipeline ribbon has zero items in `Blocked` for more than 48 hours.
- Every project has a `deadline` and a `client_id`.
- No billable expense is older than seven days without an invoice.
- Tool burn is &lt; 8% of monthly revenue.

That's the bar. Everything else is decoration.
